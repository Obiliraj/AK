How many players?

Tap your picture to change it, and type your name.

📜 How to play
  1. Shuffle and place: The cards are shuffled and the deck goes face down in the middle.
  2. Ask the question: Draw a card and read the two choices out loud.
  3. Choose and share: Every player picks Option A or Option B and explains why. There are no right or wrong answers!
  4. Pass the turn: Pass the deck to the next player and play again.
